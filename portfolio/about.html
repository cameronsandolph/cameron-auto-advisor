About

Hi, I'm Cameron.

I work where cars and finance meet, and my job is to make that part simple.

Most people only negotiate a vehicle deal every few years. The other side of the table does it every day. I try to even that out: I explain how a payment is actually built, flag the line items that matter, and help people decide with full information.

I also build software for this. The Auto Advisor estimator started as a personal spreadsheet and grew into a full calculator for leases, loans, taxes and long-term ownership costs.

What I focus on

  • Lease structure: money factor, residual value, cap cost and signing cash
  • Finance: APR, term, amortization and the true cost of interest
  • Taxes and fees, stated clearly and checked against the contract
  • Lease-vs-buy and long-term ownership comparisons
